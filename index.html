I build software that heals,

At ten, a film inspired by Asimov's Bicentennial Man, a robot building synthetic organs on his way to becoming human, sparked a dream: technology that heals from within.

Biomedical engineering gave that dream a name. Today I build healthcare software, VR and AI systems that bridge the gap between human biology and technology.

When I'm not shipping medical apps, I'm simulating evolution, fluid physics, or throwing themed house parties, chasing the idea that the most beautiful technology is the kind that makes us more human.
